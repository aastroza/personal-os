# Weekly Note — template

The **input** of the weekly planning method: fill this each week (e.g. as a phone note titled
"WEEK <date>"). The scheduled planner reads it to draft next week. Today it's a note; tomorrow
it can be the **front-end form of an app** — the methodology stays the same.

Keep it short and honest. The reflection (2) and energy (1) matter as much as the goals — they're
what make next week's plan smarter than this one.
